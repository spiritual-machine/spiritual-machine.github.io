Improving the Boba Experience

Summary

In today's digital age, we are simultaneously more connected and more alone than ever. College students, especially students from abroad, struggle to socialize in a culture they are acclimating to. Our goal was to design a solution to help foster new friendships between students on Purdue's main campus. Our methods included interviews, surveys, and physical prototyping. Our solution was a card game with personal questions of increasing depth with a whiteboard to indicate availability- this solution aims to be placed in public study spaces or in coffee shops.

Details

  • Survey – Our survey investigated sentiments around what people value in friendships and the feelings surrounding ease of socialization on Purdue's main campus. We found that the average rating (1 = disagree, 5 = agree) for feelings of community and having many friends was close to 3.37; ratings of loneliness were close to 3.
  • Literature review – Our literature review covered many theories of how friendships are formed and maintained, including how social behavior is driven by a push-and-pull dynamic between avoidance and social-seeking behaviors.
  • Interviews - The interviews we conducted showed us that initiating a new social interaction was the primary "bottleneck" and that friendships were valued based on trust.
  • Affinity diagramming – We used an affinity diagram to synthesize from our data collection,
  • Evaluation – We evaluated our design by testing it at a local coffee shop and asking participants to do a post-activity reflection. We found that card sequencing was necessary (building in depth, so some rapport is established first and foremost) and that the card game was effective as a light-weight addition to a social coffeeshop experience.

Final Results

Our final solution was a card game with questions that increase in depth and intimacy. Users can write something about themself on a prompted whiteboard (i.e. "My name is ___ and I study ___") to indicate availability to sit with other students that may be looking for a spot.
