Human-Robot Interaction with Starship

Summary

Digital distractions pose a barrier to the uninterrupted attention that many tasks (work, study) require. Our aim was to prototype an intervention to assist in mitigating digital distractions for graduate students and young professionals. To do this, we leveraged primary and secondary research methods and used iterative design processes. The final ideas were evaluated using a scoring matrix.

Details

  • Initial survey – We created a survey to assess students’ sentiments towards Starship robots. We specifically focused on emotions such as trust/distrust and like/dislike towards the delivery bots.
  • Interviews – We conducted two interviews with Purdue’s emergency management team and conducted two types of brief interview campaigns with random students found walking around campus. Questions were oriented towards preparedness need, assessing students’ weather experiences and awareness, and sentiments towards Starship.
  • Literature review – Similar to project 1, we conducted a literature review starting with three primary research questions and creating search strings to answer them.
  • Affinity diagramming –We created an affinity diagram from the data collected from surveys, interviews, and the literature review to synthesize final conclusions.
  • Ideation – Nine ideas were formed with iteration done as obvious areas for improvement were identified.
  • User personas – We created two user personas focusing on two elements of the interaction: one persona focused on the experience of a student walking outside before a storm; the other persona centered around the experience of a student purchasing food from the Starship app.
  • Evaluation – We evaluated our idea using user feedback. We created a low-fidelity prototype of the physical system and a high-fidelity mockup of the application interface for testing. Additionally, we received positive feedback from Purdue’s emergency team.

Final Results

Our final design involves a color-coded LED strip around the crown of the Starships to warn students of incoming severe weather. Additionally, users indoors ordering food would see a dismissable flashing popup to remain indoors or take shelter.
